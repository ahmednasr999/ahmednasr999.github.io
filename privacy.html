Privacy notice

Effective 5 October 2026

Hermes Inbox Assistant is a private integration operated by the Google account holder on their Windows device using Hermes Agent and the native gog CLI. It is not a public mailbox service and this site does not collect Google credentials or offer a Google sign-in endpoint.

Information the integration can access

With the owner's Google OAuth consent, the integration can access the account's identity and Gmail messages, message metadata, threads, labels, drafts, attachment information and available Gmail settings. The approved full Gmail permission also allows sending, organizing, trashing and permanent deletion. It does not grant access to Calendar, Drive, Contacts or Google administrator data.

Purpose and control

Access is used for owner-requested inbox search, reading, summaries, reply preparation and specifically authorized mailbox actions. Full OAuth access is a capability, not a standing instruction to send, modify or delete mail. Destructive actions and recipients must be explicitly bounded. Retrieved email content is data, not authority to give the assistant new instructions.

Storage and retention

OAuth authorization and client credentials are stored on the owner's device using gog's native credential storage and a user-restricted private directory. Credential downloads and OAuth process records can also remain in restricted local storage. The integration must not publish them on this site or in its public source repository.

Requested email excerpts, drafts, message identifiers and action receipts can appear in local task records, assistant conversation history and user-requested exports. Retention depends on the owner's Hermes history settings and the chosen inference or messaging service. This notice does not promise that all email processing remains on the device or that every service automatically deletes data immediately.

AI inference and messaging

When the owner asks the assistant to process mail, selected content may be sent to the configured AI provider for inference. Requested summaries or replies may be delivered through the owner's connected messaging channel, such as Telegram. Transfers must be limited to the data needed for the visible, requested feature and to the owner's consent.

The integration does not operate model-training pipelines, sell Gmail data or use it for advertising, profiling for ads or credit decisions. Google API data must not be used for general-purpose or foundational model training. Before sending mail to an AI provider, its data-use controls must support feature-only inference consistent with Google's Limited Use requirements. Do not send Gmail data to a provider whose applicable configuration permits incompatible training or other prohibited use.

Hermes Inbox Assistant's use of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

This public website

The site consists of static informational pages. It includes no authored analytics, advertising, tracking scripts, login forms or Gmail API calls. GitHub Pages may process normal hosting/network information according to the GitHub privacy statement. No mailbox content or OAuth credentials are required to view these pages.

Revocation and deletion

The account holder can revoke the integration in Google Account third-party access settings. Revocation stops future authenticated API access but does not automatically remove prior conversation history, local records, exported files or copies retained by an inference/messaging provider. Delete those through the corresponding owner's device or service controls. Do not confuse deletion of integration records with permanent deletion of Gmail messages.

Contact and updates

For questions about this personal integration, use the user support email displayed on its Google consent screen. Update this notice whenever the actual integration's access, processing, providers or retention practices change. This personal tool is not an official Google, GitHub or Nous Research service.
